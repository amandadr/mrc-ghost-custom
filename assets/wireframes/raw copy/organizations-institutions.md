FAQs
Straight answers to the questions institutions ask before starting work with an independent
consultant.
Existing CMS or vendor?
Yes. I work with what you already have. If your current system is sound, we improve it. If it is not, I will
tell you plainly and explain why.
Accessibility requirements explained?
I can help you understand what standards apply to your organization. We will review your site against
them and fix the issues that matter most.
Multiple stakeholders involved?
I have worked with boards, committees, and staff teams. We establish clear roles early so decisions
happen without endless meetings.
Help before procurement?
I can help you define the problem, write a clear scope, and understand realistic costs. That way you
go to procurement with confidence.
Documentation and handoff?
You receive written guides, training sessions, and a record of every decision. Your team owns the
system from the first day.
Still have questions?
Ask me anything. No jargon, no obligation, just answers.
