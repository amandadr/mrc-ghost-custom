About
Independent by choice,
practical by nature
I started Manny Roy Consulting after years of watching small organizations
get sold technology they did not need. My work is built on a simple idea
that you should understand and control the systems that run your
business.
No account managers or handoffs. You work directly with me.
I build systems you can maintain without a developer on retainer.
Every recommendation is tied to a real business outcome.

Process
How we work together, step
by step
Services Contact
Discovery
We start with a conversation about your business, your customers, and
what is not working today. No technical talk, just honest questions.
Recommendations
I give you a clear plan with options and trade-offs. You decide what fits
your budget and your goals.
Implementation
I build or fix what we agreed on. You see progress along the way, not a
big reveal at the end.
Knowledge transfer
I show you how to use what we built. You leave with a system you
understand and control.
A life built around good work and
open air
When I am not at the desk, I am outside with the dogs or fixing something
in the farmhouse. The same patience I bring to a slow-cooked meal goes
into the systems I build for you.
