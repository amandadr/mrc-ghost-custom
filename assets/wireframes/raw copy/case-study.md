Capabilities
What made this project work
A few practical decisions made the difference. Each one was chosen to fit the inn's needs, not to
impress with technology.
Services Contact
Platform architecture
We chose a reliable booking platform and connected it cleanly to the website. The
system was simple to manage and easy to maintain.
Accessibility improvements
Clear fonts, strong contrast, and keyboard-friendly forms made the site usable for
every guest, including older visitors.
Technical SEO
Clean page titles, fast load times, and mobile-friendly design helped more travelers
find the inn through search.
