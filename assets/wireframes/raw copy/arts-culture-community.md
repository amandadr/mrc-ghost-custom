Shared access
No single login, no single point of failure. Everyone who needs it gets
the right access.
Clear roles
Editors, approvers, and administrators. Each person knows exactly what
they can do.
Simple management
Content updates that take minutes, not days. Built for people, not

Documented training

Approach
We build it together, then

Services Contact
Listen first
We start with your goals, your audience, and the real constraints of your
team. The plan follows from there.
Build together
You are involved throughout. I explain decisions in plain language so
nothing feels like a black box.
Train your people
Before launch, your staff and volunteers practice updating content. You
leave with clear documentation and no fear.
Stay independent
The goal is self-governance. You should never need to call a developer
to change an event time or add a photo.
